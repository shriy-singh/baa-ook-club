Privacy policy

Baa-ook Club browser extension and website · Last updated 4 November 2026 (website free trial and data page added)

In short: Baa-ook Club has no servers of its own that store your data, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. To read your book and answer you, it sends your book and your questions to Google's Gemini API. Please don't upload anything sensitive, confidential or personal.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Baa-ook Club browser extension (for Firefox and other browsers) and the Baa-ook Club website at baa-ook-book-club.vercel.app. Baa-ook Club is a personal, open-source project by Shriya Singh. The code is public at github.com/shriy-singh/baa-ook-club.

What the extension handles

  • Your Gemini API key. You paste your own key from Google AI Studio. It is stored only in your browser's local extension storage on that device. It is not synced, and it is only ever sent to Google's Gemini API to authorise your own requests. You can remove it at any time with "Forget my key", or by uninstalling the extension.
  • Books you upload. The PDF goes directly from your browser to the Gemini Files API so the hosts can read it. Google keeps uploaded files for 48 hours and then deletes them.
  • Your questions and the answers. What you type (or say, see below) is sent to Gemini together with a short instruction describing the host's personality. Gemini's written answer is then sent back to Gemini's text-to-speech service to create the host's voice.
  • Your voice. If your browser supports it, the mic button uses the browser's built-in speech recognition to turn what you say into text. In Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers, that audio is processed by the browser maker's speech service. Firefox has no built-in speech recognition, so the mic button doesn't appear there and nothing is recorded.

The extension can only connect to itself and to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). It does not read the websites you visit, your tabs or your browsing history.

What Google does with it

Your book, questions and answers are processed by Google under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy, using the API key you provide.

If your key uses Gemini's free (unpaid) tier, Google says it uses the content you submit to provide, improve and develop its products, and that human reviewers may read it (after disconnecting it from your account and key). That is why you shouldn't upload sensitive, confidential or personal documents. If you are in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom, Google applies its paid-service data terms to free use as well. If your key has billing enabled, Google's paid-service terms apply.

The website version

The website works the same way, with two differences.

  • Free trial on the site's key. Each browser can use 3 books a month on the site's own Gemini key. To count them, the site sets one cookie, bc_trial, holding only the month, how many free books you've used and how many questions you've asked about the current one. It's signed so it can't be edited, contains no name or identifier, and resets each month.
  • Your country, not stored. Our host, Vercel, works out your country from your IP address on each request. The site uses it only to decide whether the free trial is allowed where you are (Gemini's terms don't allow free shared keys for public sites in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK; visitors there bring their own key). It isn't saved.
  • Your own key, if you add one. It's kept in your browser's local storage and sent to the site's server with each request, which passes it straight to Google and never stores or logs it. "Forget my key" removes it.

The server passes your book and questions to Gemini and returns the answers; it does not store them. Vercel keeps standard short-lived technical request logs (time, page, IP address). You can see, live, what your browser shares and what the site keeps on What this site knows about you.

What we never do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, analytics, tracking, fingerprinting or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone other than Google, as described above.
  • No storing of your books, questions or key on any server we run. The only thing kept is the small trial cookie described above.

Age

Google's Gemini API terms require users to be 18 or older, so Baa-ook Club is for adults only, even though the hosts are cartoon characters.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions or concerns: please open an issue at github.com/shriy-singh/baa-ook-club/issues.
